About Me

Hello! I'm John Godfrey, a Senior at Arizona State University pursuing a degree focused on Data Analytics and Data Science. I specialize in turning complex, unstructured datasets into actionable business insights and predictive statistical models using Python, SQL, R, and Tableau.

My passion lies at the intersection of statistical analysis, machine learning architecture, and database management. Whether building neural networks in PyTorch, mapping demographic vulnerability against environmental metrics, or architecting database systems in Supabase, I approach data problems with strong analytical rigor and practical business sense.

Outside of data analysis, I am a devoted husband and father. I also actively serve my community through volunteer work with my local church, which grounds my commitment to leadership, integrity, and strong communication in everything I do.
